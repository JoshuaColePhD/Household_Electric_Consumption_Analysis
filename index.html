Interpretation

What this says to a recruiter

The project does more than train a model: it compares against strong baselines, respects time ordering, and shows when added model complexity is justified.

  • Persistence is hard to beat at 1 minute because nearby readings are highly autocorrelated.
  • At 15 and 60 minutes, engineered temporal features help Random Forest degrade more gracefully.
  • The dashboard productizes the analysis so results are easy to inspect and explain.
